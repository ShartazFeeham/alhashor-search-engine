## Notes

Line 10 carries the owner's note about Najd. Lines 10 and 11 are two Bukhari entries of the same Ibn Umar report (Bukhari 6613 and 980); no other report of the Sham/Yemen blessing is in the data. Last line reuses hadis chosen for sets 1-2 (wasila: Bukhari 955); alternates for the same line are Muslim 2131 (visiting graves) and Muslim 5847 (tabarruk).
